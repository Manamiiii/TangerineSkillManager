# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors and users regardless of experience, identity or background.

Be respectful, patient and specific. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Give credit, accept constructive feedback, and help keep discussions focused on the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces representing this project.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and warn, temporarily restrict or permanently exclude participants whose conduct harms the community. Actions should be proportional to the behavior and protect affected participants' privacy.

Report concerns to the repository owner through an available private contact channel on their public profile. Do not post private details in a public issue. A dedicated project reporting contact can be added by the owner before a broader community launch.
